Kobe Bean Bryant (August 23, 1978 – January 26, 2020) was an American professional basketball player. Nicknamed "the Black Mamba," he played his entire 20-year NBA career for the Los Angeles Lakers

winning five championships, an MVP award, and two Olympic gold medals, and is widely considered one of the greatest players in sports history.

Early Life and Career Beginnings

Born in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, to former NBA player Joe "Jellybean" Bryant.

Spent his childhood in Italy where his father played professional basketball.

Rose to fame at Lower Merion High School in Pennsylvania, bypassing college to enter the 1996 NBA Draft directly.

Drafted 13th overall by the Charlotte Hornets and traded to the Los Angeles Lakers
